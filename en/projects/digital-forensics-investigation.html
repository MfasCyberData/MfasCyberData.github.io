01 / Context

The academic NARCOS-2019 scenario provides a clear thread: collect digital traces, cross-check evidence across devices and reconstruct a reasoned timeline.

The scenario focused on a drug-trafficking operation between Australia and New Zealand. Three digital systems were provided for analysis: John Fredricksen’s laptop, Jane Esteban’s laptop and Steve Kowhai’s desktop.

The goal was not only to extract files. The work was to connect artefacts, understand each actor’s role and rebuild a coherent, defensible narrative.

04 / Investigation findings and conclusions

Evidence from three devices establishes the role of each participant in the NARCOS-2019 scenario.

John Fredricksen · coordinationClient records, shipment files and messages reconstruct the trafficking operation.

On JOHNFLAPTOP1, clients.ods lists clients and associates, shipping.png documents an earlier shipment, and Steve_K.png corroborates travel arrangements to Wellington. The encrypted Memo_Things.odt describes smuggling strategies; Discord messages (000013.log) show coordination with Steve Kowhai and pressure exerted on Jane Esteban.

Steve Kowhai · local logisticsMessages and concealed instructions connect him to distribution in New Zealand.

On SK-DESKTOP, Discord exchanges cross-checked against John's records refer to the planned handover locations: Eastbourne Library and 666 Rewera Avenue, Petone. Examining BNE.png recovered the hidden image package.jpg, containing instructions about concealing a parcel. Deleted narcotics-related files and images associated with the Mongrel Mob further support his role as a local distributor.

Jane Esteban · undercover operationAFP documents, OneNote entries and Quasar artefacts explain the surveillance activity.

On JELAPTOP, Australian Federal Police documents, OneNote entries and Discord conversations support her preparation for an undercover operation within the scenario. The Quasar v1.3.0.0 folder, Contact_Card.zip archive and logs linked to JOHNFLAPTOP1 document the surveillance setup; the report also records active Quasar traces in John's laptop memory.

Cross-device corroborationCombined evidence explains the events, rather than merely listing recovered artefacts.

Discord caches from John, Steve and Jane, flight bookings, recovered documents, steganography artefacts and metadata support the same sequence: planning, coordinating transport, concealing instructions and interception on 16 February 2019. Their correlation supports the distinction between the organiser, the New Zealand logistical contact and the undercover operative.

05 / Timeline

From late-January preparations to the interception on 16 February 2019.

We cross-referenced Discord messages, browser histories, Quasar logs, recovered files and metadata to sequence the actions and compare evidence across the three devices.

  • 29–31 January: Jane prepares her undercover identity; exchanges among John, Jane and Steve cover contacts, flights and shipment logistics.
  • 30 January – 2 February: Quasar logs recovered from JELAPTOP refer to the JOHNFLAPTOP1 client; related traces are also examined in John's memory.
  • 1 February: messages discuss steganography and sending concealed instructions, cross-referenced with BNE.png recovered from SK-DESKTOP.
  • 16 February: travel records and logistics messages align with the journey and interception in Wellington described by the scenario.

06 / Complementary labs

The focused labs follow the same logic: start from a technical trace and explain what it establishes.

Windows forensicsRebuild activity from the MFT and browser artefacts

MFTECmd, Timeline Explorer and Autopsy recover a downloaded ZIP, its origin through Zone.Identifier, then related browsing and autofill traces.

Linux memory forensicsConnect the malicious binary to execution and persistence

Memory analysis identifies youtube_downloader.elf, its processes, the persistence path /usr/bin/nettstat, remote IP 64.23.144.215 and attacker-created account systemservice. A separate exercise in the same lab covered LiME memory acquisition, shown below.

Network forensicsAttribute activity by combining independent network signals

In the Nitroba PCAP, source IP, DNS queries, HTTP cookies and MAC address converge on Johnny Coach as the sender of the harassing emails.

07 / Conclusion

The investigation reconstructs a trafficking operation, its local logistics and an undercover surveillance effort.

Client lists, shipment records, Discord conversations, instructions extracted from BNE.png and Quasar artefacts do not describe isolated events: correlating them reconstructs the relationships among the participants and preparations for transport to New Zealand.

What this brings to my profile

NARCOS-2019 and the complementary labs taught me to turn scattered technical artefacts into a coherent timeline and an evidence-based explanation. Evidence integrity, cross-checking and careful interpretation matter as much as the tools used to extract it.

This experience strengthens my approach to data more generally: verify the source, preserve traceability, distinguish observation from interpretation, and draw only the conclusions the evidence can actually support.
